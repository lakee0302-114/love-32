Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026. Published by Luvio Technologies Pte. Ltd.

1. Data Protection & Zero-Knowledge Architecture

Love32 Ultra is engineered for maximum couple privacy. All telemetry, location coordinates, notes, touch signals, and intimacy widgets are transmitted over encrypted TLS 1.3 and stored using AES-256 encryption. Only you and your connected partner hold the keys to view shared telemetry.

2. Information We Process

  • Account Details: Your registered email and chosen avatar for authentication.
  • Partner Pairing Telemetry: Battery status, network state, and live distance calculated strictly between the two paired devices.
  • Audio & Touch Signals: Real-time WebSocket triggers delivered ephemerally without persistent audio recording.

3. Your Rights & Data Deletion

In full compliance with GDPR and international data standards, you retain the right to delete your account, wipe all stored couple history, or revoke device pairing instantly via the in-app settings menu.

4. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries or data requests, contact our Data Protection Officer at: privacy@love32.app
